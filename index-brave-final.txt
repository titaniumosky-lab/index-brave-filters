! Title: Index.hr - Brave Android FINAL FAIL-CLOSED AD PERIMETER
! Version: 2026-09-17.1-FAIL-CLOSED
!
! Goal: do not chase individual ad vendors. Instead, close the delivery
! paths that can carry or execute advertising on Index.hr, while keeping
! Index.hr first-party page resources as intact as practical.
!
! IMPORTANT DESIGN LIMIT:
! A static content blocker cannot mathematically guarantee removal of an
! ad that is rendered entirely as first-party HTML/content with no ad-like
! marker. The rules below therefore use a fail-closed network perimeter:
! unknown third-party delivery domains do not need to be known in advance,
! and first-party frame / external-connect paths used by ad loaders are
! also constrained. This is intentionally more aggressive than vendor
! chasing and can affect legitimate third-party embeds on Index.hr.

! 8) FAIL-CLOSED: UNKNOWN THIRD-PARTY AD DELIVERY
! ============================================================
! This is the key change. Any future vendor/domain is caught automatically
! as long as the ad payload is delivered as a third-party resource.
! $important is intentional so site-specific exceptions elsewhere do not
! silently re-open the perimeter.

*$3p,subdocument,important,domain=index.hr|www.index.hr
*$3p,xhr,important,domain=index.hr|www.index.hr
*$3p,websocket,important,domain=index.hr|www.index.hr
*$3p,image,important,domain=index.hr|www.index.hr

! ============================================================
! 9) FAIL-CLOSED: FIRST-PARTY / SAME-SITE FRAME DELIVERY
! ============================================================
! Prevent an unknown first-party or same-site iframe from becoming an
! ad delivery container. This is deliberately stricter than the normal
! 3p rules above.
*$subdocument,important,domain=index.hr|www.index.hr

! Defence in depth: page-level CSP prevents any frame navigation, including
! if a request later becomes exempted by another filter source.
||index.hr^$csp=frame-src 'none'
||index.hr^$csp=object-src 'none'

! ============================================================
! 10) FAIL-CLOSED: EXTERNAL AD AUCTION / DELIVERY CONNECTIONS
! ============================================================
! Block future unknown third-party fetch/XHR/WebSocket delivery without
! needing to know the destination domain in advance.
||index.hr^$csp=connect-src 'self' https://*.index.hr
