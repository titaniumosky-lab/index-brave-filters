! ============================================================
! ANTI-ADBLOCK RECOVERY — ERROR-REPORT.COM
! ============================================================
! Index.hr can surface the generic error-report.com recovery page
! when ad-recovery/loader logic detects that an ad dependency was
! blocked. Suppress the recovery injector and its third-party frame.

! ============================================================
! STRUCTURAL REDIRECTOR DEFENSE — DOMAIN AGNOSTIC
! ============================================================
! Blocks third-party document/navigations from Index.hr when the
! destination URL itself contains an embedded Index.hr host marker.
! This catches rotating ad/redirect domains without maintaining a
! hostname list for each new campaign domain.
/^https?:\/\/[^/]+\/[^\r\n]*\/www\.index\.hr(?:[/?]|$)/$document,3p,important,domain=index.hr

! Also stop subframe versions of the same redirect pattern.
/^https?:\/\/[^/]+\/[^\r\n]*\/www\.index\.hr(?:[/?]|$)/$subdocument,3p,important,domain=index.hr
